# SPX JEV changes, 2026-09-29

134 changes (one line per change; none were exact repeats).

## Questions JEV is asked

- The round-number question pauses when no level is in play, instead of repeating an old answer.
- The strike-defense question pauses when no contested strike is within reach.
- The opening-pace choices now use the same small, middle and large bands as the range facts.
- The gap question pauses on mornings with no real gap, instead of reporting missing data.
- The "loaded ruler" choices name the Fed events behind it and say when none are today.
- A wall touch not yet judged can no longer borrow an earlier, unrelated answer.
- Six questions whose data is never saved are marked waiting, each naming what it needs.
- The "day's character" question is first asked at 11:02, once it can be measured.
- The size-leadership question gains a "one ahead, one behind" choice.
- Calendar questions pause on ordinary days instead of answering "nothing scheduled" every read.
- The opening run asks the event-clock question again at 10:05, after the 10:00 releases.
- The overnight-range facts are listed as "not built yet", not "waiting for data".
- A once-a-day question that got no answer is asked again at the next read.
- An answer given before a question paused is never reused afterwards.
- The 10:32 read waits for a full first hour of data, as 10:02 waits for half.
- Questions marked "never reuse" never get an old answer carried forward.
- The opening-pace and loaded-ruler choice checks exist once each again, not twice.
- A pause rule can now say "could not measure" instead of simply pausing.
- Questions whose pause condition cannot be measured now show as missing data, not paused.
- The closing-window questions pause on half days, naming the 1:00 pm close.
- A waiting question carries its own reason onto the card and into the log.
- The settle-pull question shows missing data, not paused, when its position cannot be ranked.

## What JEV is told (facts)

- The put-tilt fact uses the answer's own words, so a flat tilt no longer reads as steep.
- The half-hour swing fact names its answer and says "no real move" only when truly small.
- The standing glossary no longer defines RSI by fixed overbought and oversold lines.
- The NYSE net-volume change and VIX change facts are renamed for what they measure.
- A "burst" is judged against how far the half hour travelled, so choppy periods are not bursts.
- An armed breakout already through its level says so, instead of "expires if not through".
- The breadth-flip fact waits out a release's first reaction; no change leans neither way.
- The book's side change is measured against yesterday's close, and its choice renamed to match.
- The average-price request says its average is minute closing prices, not the volume-weighted average.
- The day-range position says "at or past" its band lines, matching the fresh-extreme fact.
- A wall touch just past the rule shows an extra decimal, so it never looks equal.
- The fact groups share one copy of common helpers, so "ruler estimated" reads the same everywhere.
- An unused VWAP touch allowance is retired.
- JEV's inputs call the volume-weighted average "VWAP", never "the day's average price".
- The Fed-speaker fact takes a speech's length from one shared setting, not its own copy.

## Scoring and learning

- A tie between a view and "unsure" now picks unsure, so ties are never graded as calls.
- After a baseline refit, each read's snapshot starts without the old baseline's calibration.
- The weights file shows each sum's hit rate on real calls beside the strict rate.
- Each learning loop names its reference odds by their true source, frozen or live.
- The average-price learner counts only reads it learned from; the call keeps its own record.
- A call lacking end-price answers is still graded and learned from on its average price.
- Once promoted, the average-price learner decides the call; the end-price learner affects only its sums.
- A call with only an average-price answer is listed, tallied and learned from like any other.
- The call shows the average-price learner's choice, using the exact blend until promotion.
- A new read-only check compares JEV's past picks with what each question's facts implied.
- Reads taken during a Fed speaker's remarks are kept out of what the learner learns.
