## 2. Confirmed findings

### Breaks something

**B1. The learning store never records the phone's call or its grade.** `spx_jev/store.py` never mentions "average" or "integral". `_call_rows` (store.py:617-651) only loops over the end-price horizons, `_grade_rows` only reads grades.jsonl, and no table reads `integral_grades.jsonl`. The one archive copy of the average-price grade is inside close_out records, which store.py:47-48 skips because they "restate the calls and grades already stored", and that is no longer true. The columns README.md:346 calls "what the card showed" (`shown_pick`, `shown_probs`, store.py:639) hold the end-price sum, not what the phone shows. Evidence: the 09-29 rebuild in scratch shows tape 09:40 with `shown_pick='unsure'` and abstained, while the phone showed "Flat 81%" graded Wrong. Tape 09:45 and 09:50 show 'unsure' in the store and 'down' on the card. The store job's first run is tonight at 16:40 ET. Fix: add the average-price call (pick, odds, edge, window, sum id) as calls rows or columns, add an answers row for its question, `average_error` on reads, and an integral-grades table. Correct store.py:47-48 and README:294/346, bump `store.SCHEMA_VERSION` to 3 and backfill. Optionally, have `integral_run` archive each average-price grade as its own record (grade.py:517 archives end-price grades only).

**B2. If only the end-price request fails, the phone shows an average-price call that is never graded or listed.** When the end-price reply fails, `hour_summary` returns an error with no `by` block (service.py:777-782), but `with_average` still attaches a good average. The phone's `callSum` (runtime/viewstation/static/m/jev-spx.html:511) then uses that average as the headline. Three things drop it after that: the grader skips records with no `by` (grade.py:488), `day_calls` skips records with no pick (service.py:447-449, and the docstring at :412 says so), and `premarket.newest_call` / `story_so_far` require `rec['pick']` (premarket.py:379, 391). So the call on screen never reaches the calls list, the tally, the average-price grade or either loop. On 09-28, JEV timeouts hit single requests one at a time, which is exactly this pattern. Fix: make a readable average answer a call in its own right and grade it from its own window. Or have the phone show the error and mark the headline "will not be graded".
