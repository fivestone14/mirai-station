Reading the card

JEV answers small questions about labelled facts, one probability per option. A probability here is not a forecast of where price goes. The sums are forecasts, graded by the bars 30 and 60 minutes later; the dashed questions are shadow questions, asked and logged, never graded and never weighted. A call is a forecast, not a trade signal, graded on the average price over its window: where each minute closed against the price at the read, averaged, in index points, and set against a flat band (the ± figure) narrower than an end price would get, because an average swings less than any one price. Its direction decides right or wrong; its size at the end price, where price stood highest and lowest (“min 3” is the window’s third minute) and any sharp move are said beside it, and an unsure call is passed, never counted as a miss. For now each call also shows the old grade, on the end price alone, and a call the average could not grade stands on that, said so. The call is JEV’s answer to that question itself: where the average will sit, up, flat or down within the ± figure, with no unsure; the old question on the end price is still asked and graded beside it, and shown muted as “End-price question”. When a sum says blended, it is JEV's sum mixed half and half with how often this time of day ended up, down or flat over recent SPX sessions. Through the first hour the opening lane reads every 5 minutes and its call, on the average price over the next 10 minutes, leads the page until its last call closes; then the 30-minute call leads again. Each call looks ahead a fixed number of minutes, so a new call can start before the last one is graded: the calls in play show that on one clock. Before the open, the pre-market lane reads S&P futures, 10-year bond futures, bitcoin futures and the morning’s reports through the night and makes one call about the first half hour: the average price over the 30 minutes after the first settled price, four minutes after the open. It is a forecast too, graded by the bars. Every time here is in this phone's own time zone.
