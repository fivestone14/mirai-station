Reading the card

JEV answers small questions about labelled facts, one probability per option. A probability here is not a forecast of where price goes. The sums are forecasts, graded by the bars 30 and 60 minutes later; the dashed questions are forecasts too, logged and never graded. Neither is a call. When a sum says blended, it is JEV's sum mixed half and half with how often this time of day ended up, down or flat over recent sessions, because on its first 37 graded reads JEV's sum alone did worse than the time of day alone. From 09:35 the opening lane reads every 5 minutes and its 10-minute call, sized in tape units, leads the page until its last call closes at 10:40; then the 30-minute call leads again. Each call looks ahead a fixed number of minutes, so a new call can start before the last one is graded: the calls in play show that on one clock.
